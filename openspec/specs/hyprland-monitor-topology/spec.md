# Hyprland Monitor Topology

## Purpose

Workspace placement SHALL follow the monitor topology by role rather than by connector
name: workspaces 1–7 SHALL live on whichever external monitor is connected, workspaces
8, 9, and 0 SHALL stay on the laptop panel, focus SHALL default to the external monitor
when present, and the binding SHALL be reconciled on hotplug and at startup so it stays
correct alongside nwg-displays-owned monitor geometry.

## Requirements

### Requirement: external-workspaces-follow-external-monitor

Workspaces 1–7 SHALL be placed on the connected external monitor (any monitor whose
name is not `eDP-1`), regardless of that monitor's connector name (e.g. `DP-2`, `DP-3`)
or hardware identity.

#### Scenario: external monitor connected as DP-2

- **WHEN** the only external monitor is connected and enumerates as `DP-2`
- **THEN** workspaces 1–7 SHALL be located on `DP-2`

#### Scenario: external monitor connected as DP-3

- **WHEN** the only external monitor is connected and enumerates as `DP-3`
- **THEN** workspaces 1–7 SHALL be located on `DP-3`

#### Scenario: different physical external monitor at a different location

- **WHEN** a different external monitor (different make/model/serial) is connected
- **THEN** workspaces 1–7 SHALL be located on that monitor without any per-location
  configuration change

### Requirement: laptop-workspaces-stay-on-panel

Workspaces 8, 9, and 0 SHALL remain on the laptop panel `eDP-1`.

#### Scenario: external present

- **WHEN** an external monitor is connected alongside the laptop
- **THEN** workspaces 8, 9, 0 SHALL be on `eDP-1` and workspaces 1–7 SHALL be on the
  external monitor

### Requirement: default-focus-on-external-when-present

When an external monitor is present, the default workspace and new-window placement SHALL target the external monitor rather than the laptop panel.

#### Scenario: opening an app with external connected

- **WHEN** an external monitor is connected
- **AND** the user opens a new application without first focusing a specific monitor
- **THEN** the application SHALL open on the external monitor's active workspace, not
  centered on the laptop panel

### Requirement: reconcile-on-hotplug-and-startup

The workspace→monitor binding SHALL be reconciled both when a monitor is added or
removed at runtime, and once at session startup, so the binding is correct without
requiring a relogin.

#### Scenario: monitor hotplugged after login

- **WHEN** an external monitor is connected after the session has already started
- **THEN** workspaces 1–7 SHALL move to the external monitor automatically

#### Scenario: external already connected at login

- **WHEN** the external monitor is already connected before the session starts
- **THEN** the binding SHALL be reconciled on startup so workspaces 1–7 are on the
  external monitor without manual intervention

#### Scenario: external monitor removed

- **WHEN** the external monitor is disconnected
- **THEN** workspaces 1–7 SHALL fall back to the laptop panel `eDP-1`

### Requirement: coexist-with-nwg-displays

The runtime workspace→monitor binding SHALL remain correct even after `monitors.conf`
and `workspaces.conf` are regenerated by nwg-displays. nwg-displays owns monitor
geometry; the runtime reconciliation owns the workspace→monitor binding.

#### Scenario: workspaces.conf regenerated with a stale connector name

- **WHEN** `workspaces.conf` pins workspaces 1–7 to a connector name that does not match
  the currently connected external monitor
- **THEN** the runtime reconciliation SHALL still place workspaces 1–7 on the connected
  external monitor
